Rwanda enacted **Law n° 011/2026 of 26/02/2026** on competition and consumer protection, replacing Law nº 36/2012. Directly relevant to RMF premium/sponsored features:

- First-ever explicit obligations for **e-commerce enterprises and online intermediaries**: mandatory disclosure of self-identifying info, registration numbers, physical addresses; rules on digital distance contracts; a **7-day right of withdrawal** on online purchases.
- Consumers have a right to "clear, accurate and complete facts" and protection against "dishonest or misleading information" — this is the hook that requires **sponsored/paid placement to be visibly labelled** (e.g. "Sponsored"/"Promoted") so a paid ranking is not misleading.
- **Grace period: 6 months from 2026-03-04** for existing operators to conform. RMF (rwshop.org) is an existing operator → conformance deadline ~2026-09-04.

**Why:** Surfaced in Phase 3 research for the premium seller/market advertising feature.
**How to apply:** Any sponsored seller/market placement MUST carry a clear "Sponsored" label and must not suppress the consumer's ability to see organic/accurate options. Treat the 2026-09 deadline as a compliance constraint when scheduling the premium-seller rollout.
